# Security Policy

## Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security issue in this repository, please report it privately to the maintainers.

- Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly before maintainers confirm remediation readiness.
- Include reproduction details, affected scope, and potential impact.
- If possible, include proposed mitigations.

Contact route and escalation contacts are TBD by governance/policy.

## Severity Classes and Response Expectations

Severity classes are guidance only and are not legal guarantees.

- **Critical**: direct compromise or systemic integrity risk. Initial acknowledgement target: within 24 hours.
- **High**: significant security impact with plausible exploitation path. Initial acknowledgement target: within 3 business days.
- **Medium**: meaningful but constrained security impact. Initial acknowledgement target: within 5 business days.
- **Low**: limited exploitability or low impact. Initial acknowledgement target: within 10 business days.

Remediation targets and disclosure timelines are TBD by governance/policy.
